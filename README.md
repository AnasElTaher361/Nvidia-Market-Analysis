📌 Executive Summary

This project presents an in-depth Power BI analysis of the NVIDIA Customer Market Dataset, designed to uncover key insights into sales performance, customer behavior, market share, and competitive dynamics. The dashboard transforms raw data into interactive visual storytelling, enabling data-driven decision-making across products, regions, and customer segments.

🎯 Business Problem

The dataset reflects a highly competitive technology market where understanding:

Sales performance trends
Customer satisfaction and retention
Regional performance differences
Competitor influence
AI/ML adoption impact

is critical for strategic decision-making.
The challenge was to structure and visualize this data to identify growth opportunities and performance bottlenecks.

🧩 Methodology

The project followed a structured BI workflow:




🔹 Data Modeling
Built a Star Schema model
Central fact table: NVIDIA Market Analysis
Connected dimensions:
Product & Region
Sales & Units Sold
Discount & Region
Return Rate & Region
Customer Segment, Product, Region

🔹 Data Visualization (Power BI)

Created multiple interactive dashboard pages:

Overview Dashboard: KPIs, revenue trends, AI/ML adoption, market share
Sales & Competitor Analysis: Line, area, ribbon, and column charts
Regional Performance: Waterfall, bar, and return rate analysis
Customer Insights: Scatter plots, satisfaction, retention metrics
Donut Charts Page: Segment-wise breakdowns of key KPIs
Advanced Insights Page:
Decomposition tree (AI/ML adoption)
Ad campaign effectiveness
Revenue performance gauge

📊 Results & Business Recommendations
📈 Key Findings
Revenue trends show clear yearly growth patterns influenced by product categories
Certain regions outperform others in both sales and market share
Competitor influence significantly impacts unit sales and satisfaction levels
AI/ML adoption correlates with higher market performance
Customer satisfaction varies strongly across product categories and regions
